Visitors are always welcome

We are glad you are thinking of joining us. It is not only great to increase our numbers, but it also increases the fun.

When we meet

We meet most Friday nights. Doors open at 6:30 PM. BOG meetings begin at 7:15 PM and most other meetings at 7:30 PM; special events can differ, as shown in the schedule.

Programs by our members, auctions, bourses (like a flea market for stamps) and other club activities are held as shown in the schedule, on our website and in The Philatex. Come early on auction nights to look over the lots.

Where we meet

MacArthur Park Lutheran Church, Building 1
2903 Nacogdoches Road
San Antonio, TX 78217

The church is on the southwest corner of Nacogdoches Road and Lovelace, about a mile north of Loop 410. Park on the Nacogdoches side and enter Building 1, which faces Nacogdoches Road. There is a big number 1 on the front of the building.

Why join

Members may sell at our auctions and bourses; visitors may only buy. Members also receive The Philatex, our club newsletter, which is published regularly and includes the club calendar. We encourage junior members to join, too.

How to join

  1. Fill in the application on the other side, on your computer or by hand.
  2. Write a check payable to S.A.P.A. for the dues shown for the quarter you apply in.
  3. Mail both by US mail to Al Lozano, Vice President, or bring them to a scheduled meeting. We do not take payments online.
