## S2: broken or misleading features

**Contact and site-wide (E2):**
- **C-1:** With no JS, the "Thank you" notice sits under the 94px sticky header. The general cause: every #fragment target lands under the sticky header (glossary A–Z, resources). Fix site-wide with `scroll-padding-top` / `scroll-margin-top`.
- **C-2:** A retired 2025 service worker survives on browsers that visited membership or glossary between 2025-09-27 and 2025-11-18, and pins a stale "Fourth Quarter 2025" home page. Fix: deploy a kill-switch `sw.js` that calls `skipWaiting`, deletes the `sapa-*` caches, unregisters itself and reloads its clients. Note: `check-site-build` and `lib/site.js` currently forbid deploying `sw.js`.
- **C-3:** Error reporting is dead. `logger.error` POSTs to `/api/logs`, which returns 405 and puts a console error in front of visitors. `window.onerror` and `onunhandledrejection` are never wired.

**Meetings (E1):**
- **M-1:** No per-meeting "Add to Calendar" control, although the home page and meetings.html promise one. 89 per-meeting `.ics` files are deployed with nothing linking to them.
  - Cause: `meetings.js:190,222` only renders the link when a meeting has `calendarLink`; no record does.
- **M-2:** The calendar doesn't mark meeting days (`meetings.js:78-96`). Clicking a day does open the right meeting.
- **M-3 (RSVP, DECISION pending):** RSVP can't be undone after a reload, because the click handler checks `rsvp-active` but a reload sets `rsvp-confirmed`. With storage blocked it throws on load. RSVP is localStorage-only.
- **M-4:** The modal title is dark on dark blue, about 1.3:1. The global heading colour overrides it; `.modal-title` sets no colour.
- **Set Reminder (DECISION pending):** throws `setReminder is not a function`, so the button does nothing.

**Search and newsletter (E3):**
- **S-1:** No search result lands on its item: 0 of 36 followed links reach their target, and 18 point at content that isn't on the page (`scripts/build-search-index.js:64,92,120,149`).
- **S-2:** Coverage and ranking:
  - none of the 98 archive issues are indexed, so "2009" finds nothing;
  - the HTML pages aren't indexed, so "membership" and "dues" find nothing useful;
  - "Philatex" ranks Q2 2025 first;
  - past meetings outrank upcoming ones.
- **S-3:** Search dates show one day early in US time zones (`search.html:481`, UTC parse).
- **S-4:** With no JS, search.html shows dead controls and no message.
- **N-1:** There is no way to subscribe. "Learn More" goes to `newsletter.html#subscribe`, which doesn't exist (`index.html:314`).

**Glossary and resources (E4):**
- **G-1:** Glossary definitions stop opening after any search-clear, filter, sort or reset: handlers pile up (`glossary.js:350`). Term headers are divs a keyboard can't reach.
- **R-1:** Resource guides render raw Markdown: `#`, `**`, leading dashes and run-together lists (`resources.js:684-695` `formatResourceContent`).
- **R-2:** Every Scott Catalog link is dead. scottonline.com never answers. It appears at `resources.html:220`, `:247`, and in the Beginner's Guide links in `resources.json`.
- **R-3:** "United States Postal Service" links to uspcs.org, the U.S. Philatelic Classics Society (`resources.html:240`).
