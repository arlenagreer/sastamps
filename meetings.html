Subscribe to the club calendar

Subscribe once and every SAPA meeting appears in your own calendar app, with a reminder the day before and another two hours before. New meetings and schedule changes arrive automatically; you never need to download anything again.

  • Subscribe (Apple Calendar, Outlook)
  • Add to Google Calendar (opens in a new tab)

Using another app, or the button did nothing? Subscribe to this address: https://www.sastamps.org/calendar/sapa-meetings.ics. In Outlook, choose Add calendar, then Subscribe from web; in Apple Calendar on a Mac, File, then New Calendar Subscription.

Subscribed calendars check for changes about once a day. Google Calendar does not use a subscribed calendar’s own reminders, so in Google add a notification to the meetings you plan to attend.
